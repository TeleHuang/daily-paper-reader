## 摘要
机器人应能通过实验学习陌生物体的行为与交互方式，并利用这些知识进行规划。它无需从零开始：物理引擎提供了运动与接触方面的知识，但可能遗漏整个机制，例如胶水固化、水加热或风。我们提出 EMPIRIC，一种学习残差世界模型的智能体：在物理引擎上扩展缺失机制的代码。学习到的程序可以引入新的力、约束和隐状态，而贝叶斯推断从带噪观测中估计其参数和状态。由此得到的模型使智能体能够预测动作结果、选择信息量大的实验，并在预测失败时修正其假设。在五个模拟领域中，EMPIRIC 学习到可解释、可复用的模型，并且相比三个基线方法，以更少的环境交互解决了更多任务。在实体机器人上，它学习风力与多米诺骨牌质量以完成一项操作任务。网站与代码：https://yichao-liang.github.io/empiric

## Abstract
A robot should be able to learn through experiments how unfamiliar objects behave and interact, then plan with that knowledge. It need not start from scratch: physics engines supply knowledge of motion and contact, but can omit entire mechanisms, such as glue curing, water heating, or wind. We present EMPIRIC, an agent that learns a residual world model: a physics engine extended with code for the missing mechanisms. The learned programs can introduce new forces, constraints, and hidden state, and Bayesian inference estimates their parameters and states from noisy observations. The resulting model lets the agent predict the outcomes of actions, choose informative experiments, and revise its hypotheses when predictions fail. Across five simulated domains, EMPIRIC learns interpretable, reusable models, and solves more tasks with fewer environment interactions than all three baselines. On a physical robot, it learns wind forces and domino masses to solve a manipulation task. Website and code: https://yichao-liang.github.io/empiric
