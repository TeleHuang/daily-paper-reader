## 摘要
通用视觉语言模型为实现零样本机器人控制提供了一条有前景的途径：GPT-6 Astra 在开放式以及以语言或图像为条件的操作任务上表现出色，但在高精度和长时程任务上仍明显较弱。我们提出 RoboICL，一个上下文机器人控制框架，无需针对机器人进行参数更新，也无需学习型 VLA，即可缩小这些差距。RoboICL 将演示上下文（在有可用记录示例时提供这些示例）与交互记忆（累积模型自身的动作和观测结果）分离。二者都使用共享的观察—动作—回执—观察语法。为跨任务阶段保留经验，RoboICL 将采样得到的演示块与有界锚定记忆相结合。固定锚点使较早的 rollout 交互可供上下文学习使用，而最新交互则支持即时纠错。在 30 个 RoboDojo 任务上，Open 使用零样本、其他任务使用一个演示，RoboICL 在每个类别中都比官方零样本 GPT-6 Astra 提升 20–27 个进度分。它在 Memory 和 Open 上领先排行榜基线，在 Precision 上达到与最强基线相当的性能，并在 Long-Horizon 上保持竞争力。其 30 个任务的 Overall 得分为 50.64，而最强基线为 33.68。在另一个十任务子集上，RoboICL 得分为 60.60，与 π_{0.5} + GPT-6 Astra 混合方法相差 2.00 分以内。在三个真实机器人任务上，平均进度从零样本的 14.45 上升到单样本的 63.33 和三样本的 78.89。在两个开发任务上，可选的 Jev 门控动作复用将 GPT-6 Astra 调用减少 33–48%。代码见 https://github.com/Mosi-AI/RoboICL。

## Abstract
General-purpose vision-language models offer a promising way to zero-shot robot control: \gptastra{} excels at open-ended and language- or image-conditioned manipulation but remains substantially weaker on high-precision and long-horizon tasks. We introduce \emph{RoboICL}, an in-context robot-control framework that narrows these gaps without robot-specific parameter updates or a learned VLA. RoboICL separates \emph{demonstration context}, which provides recorded examples when available, from \emph{interaction memory}, which accumulates the model's own actions and observed outcomes. Both use a shared observation--action--receipt--observation grammar. To preserve experience across task stages, RoboICL combines sampled demonstration blocks with bounded anchored memory. Fixed anchors keep earlier rollout interactions available for in-context learning, while the latest interaction supports immediate error correction. Across 30 RoboDojo tasks, using zero shot for Open and one demonstration elsewhere, RoboICL improves on official zero-shot \gptastra{} by 20--27 progress-score points in every category. It leads the leaderboard baselines on Memory and Open, achieves comparable performance to the strongest Precision baseline, and remains competitive on Long-Horizon. Its 30-task Overall score is 50.64, versus 33.68 for the strongest baseline. On a separate ten-task subset, RoboICL scores 60.60, within 2.00 points of the $π_{0.5}$ + \gptastra{} hybrid approach. On three real-robot tasks, mean progress rises from 14.45 at zero shot to 63.33 at one shot and 78.89 at three shots. On two development tasks, optional Jev-gated action reuse reduces \gptastra{} calls by 33--48\%. Code is available at \href{https://github.com/Mosi-AI/RoboICL}{https://github.com/Mosi-AI/RoboICL}.
