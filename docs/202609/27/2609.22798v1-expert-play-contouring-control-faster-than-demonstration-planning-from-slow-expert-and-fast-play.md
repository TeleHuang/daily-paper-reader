## 摘要
专家示范通常指定机器人应该做什么，但并未说明它能以多快的速度完成。模仿学习（IL）继承了示范的时序，而当更快的执行改变机器人-物体动力学时，直接加速学习到的运动可能会失败。我们将快于示范的执行作为一个动力学感知的控制问题进行研究，并提出专家-游玩轮廓控制（EPCC），该方法将慢速专家示范与快速的非专家游玩相结合。专家示范训练一个潜在轨迹生成器，其预测被重新参数化为成功任务进展的与时间无关的轮廓，而游玩则训练一个快速动作结果的世界模型（WM）。在部署时，我们提出的规划器利用 WM 优化动作，以最大化沿专家导出的轮廓的进展，同时惩罚偏离预期任务演化的行为。在三个视觉运动操作任务上平均而言，EPCC 的吞吐量达到 IL 基线的 2.0 倍，其中在具有交互敏感物体动力学的任务上，其吞吐量达到最强加速基线的 2.2 倍。我们的分析表明，这些增益集中在更快的执行改变机器人-物体演化的地方。总之，我们的结果强调了一个简单而有效的原则：示范提供任务意图，而游玩数据提供更快执行该意图所需的动力学覆盖。

## Abstract
Expert demonstrations often specify what a robot should do, but not how fast it can do it. Imitation Learning (IL) inherits demonstration timing, while directly accelerating the learned motion can fail when faster execution changes the robot-object dynamics. We study faster-than-demonstration execution as a dynamics-aware control problem and introduce Expert-Play Contouring Control (EPCC), which combines slow expert demonstrations with fast, non-expert play. Expert demonstrations train a latent trajectory generator whose predictions are reparameterized into a time-independent contour of successful task progression, while play trains a world model (WM) of fast-action outcomes. At deployment, our proposed planner uses the WM to optimize actions that makes maximize progress along the expert-derived contour while penalizing deviation from the intended task evolution. Averaged across three visuomotor manipulation tasks, EPCC achieves a $2.0\times$ the throughput of the IL baseline, including $2.2\times$ that of the throughput of the strongest acceleration baseline on a task with interaction-sensitive object dynamics. Our analysis shows that the gains concentrate where faster execution changes robot-object evolution. Together, our results highlight a simple yet effective principle: demonstrations provide task intent, while play data provides the dynamic coverage needed to execute that intent faster.
